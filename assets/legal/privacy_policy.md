# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 2026**

Welcome to Confessions. We value your authentic expression and are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and security. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information, especially concerning the Google Play and Apple App Store guidelines.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Data:** When you register, we collect your Username, Email, and securely hashed passwords.
- **Audio Confessions:** We securely store audio recordings you explicitly choose to publish on our platform. 
- **Analytics & Crash Data:** We use Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics to monitor app performance and stability. This data is anonymized and used strictly to improve the app experience.

## 2. Audio Processing & Storage
Your audio confessions are processed securely. We do not use your voice data to train external AI models without your explicit, opt-in consent. If you choose to delete your account or a confession, the associated audio file is permanently purged from our active databases.

## 3. Subscriptions (Premium Access)
If you subscribe to Premium Access (₹270 / $8 per month), your payment is processed securely via the respective App Store (Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchases) managed by RevenueCat. We do not store or process raw credit card data on our servers.

## 4. User Rights and Data Deletion
In compliance with global privacy standards, you have the right to request a complete deletion of your data. You may do so directly within the App Settings or by contacting our support team.

## 5. Offline Availability
This document is bundled with the application and is fully available offline to ensure you can always review your rights.
